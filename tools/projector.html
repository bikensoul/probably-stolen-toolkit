How it's calculated

Projector bonus = 100 × (1 + 0.25 × Performance% + 0.5 × Quality%).
Energy = 16 × (1 − Efficiency%).
Your in-game values (+42% Performance, +80% Quality, −64% Efficiency) give 150.5% → shown as 150%, and 26.24 → 26 energy per night. The bonus applies the day after the projector has run overnight.
