Ribwich

Based on the Station Trade Commission guide. Anything counterfeit is worth 40 Credits.

Genuine example from the 2nd run: production date next to the barcode, packing facility on the label, Ribwich sticker and Davre logo on the bun.

1 · Packaging

2 · Production date

Valid runs: 1st 01/425 – 12/429 (+80), 2nd 01/432 – 12/438. The very first and last month, 01/425 and 12/438, are worth +50 extra.

3 · Packing facility

High quality (+50): 01, 02, 05, 08, 21, 34. Low quality (−50): 07, 15, 18, 26, 31. Valid numbers are 1 – 35.

4 · Deluxe Edition

Both together = Deluxe Edition (+300). Only one of them = counterfeit.

5 · Double meat patty

6 · Davre logo

7 · Ribwich sticker

2nd-run Ribwiches must have the genuine sticker. 1st-run Ribwiches never have one.
