Good to know

  • Your inputs are remembered in this browser, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • "Pour" lines use the in-game measuring cups (1 to 6000 mL), rounded up to whole mL.
  • Works on a phone, which is handy as a second screen while you play.
  • Next time, the toolkit opens on the tool you used last.
