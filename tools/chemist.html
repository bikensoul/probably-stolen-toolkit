Machines

  • Chem Scanner: shows the composition of a container or product.
  • Blender: upgrades improve extraction yield.
  • Heating Plate: upgrades raise max temperature, temperature control and heating speed.
  • Evaporator: works overnight to remove liquids such as water and alcohol. Uses normal modules.
  • Chem Finisher: upgrades reduce the impurity it adds.
  • Most chemistry machines can only be upgraded with the Chemical Equipment Module.
